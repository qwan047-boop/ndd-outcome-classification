# Distribution and provenance notice

The data originate from ClinicalTrials.gov, maintained by the U.S. National Library of Medicine. The acquisition-batch identifiers for the 14 archived registry pages are recorded in `data/registry_page_provenance.json`. These are historical analysis extracts, not current trial records. Where preserved by the registry, the individual posted dates and update dates appear in `registry_status_fields`. A single registry processing timestamp for this derived extract was not recovered. The current official study URL is preserved per NCT. A current API query may give different records and cannot be substituted for the frozen analyses.

The package selected only NCT and source metadata, selected registry status and eligibility fields, intervention types and names, study conditions, and the raw outcome measure, description and time-frame strings needed for this task. These strings have not been edited. It removed contact modules, other registry modules, and local file paths. Eligibility decisions, drug-role classifications, group identifiers, weak labels and human labels are research-derived fields. This extract and any future redistribution are subject to the [ClinicalTrials.gov terms](https://clinicaltrials.gov/terms-conditions), including source attribution, currency and processing-date expectations, disclosure of transformations, and third-party rights where applicable. The author has confirmed approval from the authorized project rights holder for project-derived research assets. Source registry fields retain their own applicable terms and third-party rights; the project makes no proprietary claim to them. The historical acquisition and processing-time limitations are explicitly disclosed in DATA_SOURCE_AND_TERMS.md.

The [official MiniLM model card](https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2) identifies Apache-2.0 for the original encoder. Pretrained weight files are referenced by revision and hash and are not distributed here. The two fitted classifiers and vectorizer are new reconstruction artifacts. The authorized rights holder's permission to publish the reconstructed project classifiers, original code and annotations has been confirmed. This does not assign a project reuse license or alter upstream terms. Do not apply a code license to ClinicalTrials.gov material or any other third-party material. No license is granted here.

The release candidate deliberately excludes internal conversation archives and links, project correspondence, reviewer materials, proprietary template files, workbooks with operator notes, and unrelated records. The fixed test labels are included for reproducibility of published estimates. A future independently held-out experiment must use a separate reference set.
